/04 THE HUMAN BEHIND THE SCREEN

Hi, I’m
Hesadiah.

I’m the multidisciplinary designer, creative director, and world-builder behind Planet Hezy. My work spans creative direction, digital design, interactive experiences, and visual storytelling: part clear system, part feeling you can’t quite name.

I turn expressive ideas into recognizable creative worlds, with care for the people, culture, and identity that give each project its pulse.

I’m especially interested in internet culture, fashion, beauty, music, entertainment, gaming, creative technology, identity, and making room for people who rarely see themselves centered in beautiful things.
